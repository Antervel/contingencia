# E1-12: Findings report and go/no-go

**Epic:** E1 (mta + imap prototype)
**Size:** S
**Depends on:** all above (E1-01 … E1-11)

## Context

The prototype epic E1 has gone through tickets E1-01 … E1-11. Each ticket produced runtime
findings in `docs/FINDINGS.md` and fixes in the prototype configuration, helpers and scenario
scripts. This ticket collects the consolidated findings of the epic and issues the final
**go / no-go** decision for the prototype as delivered, so that Epic 2 (DKIM, TLS, admin
service, hardening) can plan against a known baseline.

## What to do

1. Consolidate the per-ticket findings in `docs/FINDINGS.md` into a final report:
   - Hypothesis outcomes H1–H10 (PASS / FAIL / CHANGED), including fallbacks F1–F9 if triggered.
   - Final scenario-suite status (tickets E1-11): scenarios green / failing, deliberate-break
     evidence, full-suite runtime, remaining known failures.
   - Operational findings that cut across tickets (Postfix master reload segfault and the
     adopted restart-based workaround; submission restriction fix).
2. Record the final decision: **GO** or **NO-GO** for the prototype epic, with the rationale and
   the follow-ups that any GO decision leaves open.

## Acceptance criteria

- `docs/FINDINGS.md` ends with a clear, final findings report for E1-12.
- The decision (GO / NO-GO) is stated explicitly and matches the final ticket state.
- Any remaining failing scenario is either fixed before the decision, or explicitly carried as
  a known finding with a follow-up ticket.

## How to test

Not applicable (documentation / decision ticket). The final suite state is the evidence:
`prototype/tests/run-all.sh` against a running stack (`docker compose up -d --build`).

## Outputs

`docs/FINDINGS.md` (final consolidated findings and decision), ticket E1-12.
